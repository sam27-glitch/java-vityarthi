Problem Statement
Managing personal finances can be challenging for individuals who lack dedicated tools to track their income and expenses. Without an organized system, it becomes difficult to monitor spending habits, identify unnecessary expenditures, and budget effectively. Many users struggle to maintain manual records, which are often prone to errors and are inconvenient to update.

Scope of the Project
The Personal Finance Tracker is a command-line application designed to provide users with a simple and effective way to record, categorize, and review their financial transactions. It covers essential functions such as tracking income and expenses, maintaining transaction history, and generating summary reports. The project focuses on ease of use, modularity, and data persistence, suitable for all computing environments.

Target Users
The primary users are individuals seeking a lightweight and accessible solution to manage personal finances on their computers. This tool is ideal for students, young professionals, or anyone interested in monitoring personal budgets without the complexity of graphical apps or online platforms.

High-Level Features
Record income and expense transactions with details such as date, amount, category, and description.

View transaction history and filter by date or category.

Generate financial summaries including total income, total expenses, and current balance.

Data persistence through local file storage to ensure records are retained between sessions.

Simple, modular command-line interface for user interaction and extensibility.
